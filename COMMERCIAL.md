# Commercial Use

## Public demonstrator vs. commercial IBRAMIND

IBRAMIND InfraRisk AI is an open public demonstrator of an evidence-first infrastructure-risk workflow. The repository is intentionally small and reproducible.

Commercial IBRAMIND engagements may include:

- Infrastructure/project risk pilots
- Engineering evidence ingestion and traceability
- Project-controls and commercial-risk integrations
- Organization-specific risk rules and workflows
- Private model/provider integration
- Enterprise authentication and access control
- Audit trails and governance
- Private deployment and support
- Integration with IBRAMIND Engineering Intelligence

## Paid pilot

A paid pilot can be scoped around a defined project portfolio, evidence set, risk workflow, and acceptance criteria.
